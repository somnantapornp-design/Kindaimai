Privacy policy

1. Who is responsible for the data

This app is made by [name of creator / organisation] to share general knowledge with dog and cat owners. For questions about personal data, contact [contact email]

2. Data kept only on your phone

The following data is saved in the browser on the device you use (localStorage). It is not sent to the creator, and the creator cannot see it.

  • The pet type you last chose (dog or cat)
  • The pet health book, if you fill it in: your pet’s name, species, birthday or approximate age, sex, neutering, life stage, overweight/kidney disease, weight entries, vaccines, deworming/flea and tick prevention, medicines your pet receives (medicine name, how to give it as prescribed by your vet, dose times and when you tap “Given”), vet visits (date, time, clinic, reason) and due dates
  • Saved items (heart button) and recent search history
  • Copies of your requests to add foods, and your “Helpful / Not clear” taps
  • Settings, such as having read the data notice, or having turned off usage data

The pet health book (pet names, birthdays, weights, vaccines, deworming, medicines and dose history, vet visits and due dates) stays on your phone only and is never sent to the creator or anywhere else. Reminders for due dates and dose times are worked out on your device and shown only while the app is open. The app does not use push notifications. The “Add to calendar” button creates a calendar file (.ics) on your device, and the “Back up data” button creates a backup file (.json) that you save wherever you choose. The creator cannot see these files. A backup file contains all your pets’ data, so keep it somewhere safe.

You can delete this data yourself at any time on the “About the information” page > “Delete all data on this device”, or by clearing site data in your browser settings. The app works without any sign-up.

3. Data sent from your device (when the creator turns on the feedback form)

If the creator connects the app to Google Forms, the app shows you a notice before the first time, and may send the following anonymous data:

  • Feedback survey (sent only when you tap Send and tick the consent box): whether you have dogs/cats, ease-of-use score, trust score, whether you will use the app again, and any text you type yourself
  • Result ratings: the food name, the pet type, “Helpful” or “Not clear”, and any text you type yourself (if any)
  • Foods searched but not found, and requests to add foods: the search words you typed and the pet type

The app does not send your name, phone number, email, location or pet profiles, unless you type that information into a text box yourself, so please avoid typing anything that could identify you.

You can turn off automatic usage data (result ratings, foods not found, requests to add foods) on the “About the information” page > “Don’t send usage data”. The survey is sent only when you tap Send yourself.

3.1 Rehoming posts

People who post a pet for rehoming submit information through a Google Form: details and photos of the pet, province and district, and a LINE ID. Once the app team approves a post, this information, including the LINE ID, is shown publicly in the app so that interested people can get in touch. Posters must give consent before submitting. Attaching a photo through Google Forms requires signing in to a Google account, and photos are stored in the app team’s Google Drive.

Posters can ask for a post to be removed or corrected at any time at [contact email]. Posts for pets that have found a home are removed within [period]. When someone taps “I’d like to adopt”, the app may count this anonymously, as described in section 3.

4. Outside data processors

  • Google (Google Forms / Google Sheets) receives and stores responses on behalf of the creator. Google may process technical data such as IP addresses under Google’s policies.
  • Google Maps: when you tap a “Nearby services” button, the app opens Google Maps with a search such as “veterinary clinic near me”. The app does not read or send your location, but Google Maps may use your location depending on your device settings and Google’s policies.
  • Google Fonts is used to display text. Your browser downloads the font from Google.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website and may log technical access data under GitHub’s policies.
  • LINE and source links open only when you tap them yourself.

The app has no ads, uses no tracking cookies and uses no other analytics tools.

5. Purpose and retention

The creator uses data from the form only to improve the app’s content and usability, for example adding foods that people often search for and fixing unclear explanations. It is not sold or disclosed to anyone else. Data is kept for no longer than [period], then deleted or turned into summary data that cannot identify anyone.

6. Your rights under Thailand’s Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (2019)

You have the right to access, obtain a copy of, correct, delete, restrict the use of, object to the processing of and transfer your data, and to withdraw consent at any time, as well as to complain to the Office of the Personal Data Protection Committee. Because responses are anonymous, the creator may not be able to tell which response is yours. To exercise your rights, please contact [contact email] with details that help find the response, such as when you sent it and the text you typed.

7. Changes to this policy

If anything changes, the creator will update the date at the top of this page, and will tell you in the app when a change affects the data sent from your device.
