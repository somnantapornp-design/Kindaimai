1. What this app is

“Can They Eat It?” gives general knowledge about which human foods dogs or cats can eat, can eat a little, should avoid, or must never eat. It is made by [name of creator / organisation] for education. It is free, has no ads and is not sponsored by any pet food brand.

2. Not veterinary advice for your individual animal

  • Information in the app is not a diagnosis, treatment, or advice specific to your pet, and cannot replace an examination by a veterinarian.
  • The app does not give toxic amounts or medicine doses. Treat amounts are rough estimates based on general guidelines only.
  • Animals that are sick, pregnant, very young, elderly or have ongoing conditions may need different care from the information in the app.
  • If a food is not in the app, that does not mean it is safe.

3. Emergencies

If your pet eats something that may be dangerous, or shows unusual signs such as seizures, trouble breathing, being very drowsy, vomit or stool with blood, or a swollen, hard belly, contact a veterinarian or animal hospital immediately. Do not wait for the app, and do not make your pet vomit or give human medicine unless a veterinarian tells you to. Animal hospitals listed in the app (if any) are for convenience only; please check opening hours with the hospital directly.
